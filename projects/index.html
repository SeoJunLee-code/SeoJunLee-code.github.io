LOSS CURVE

AI-startup management game (desktop build)

Grow a garage startup into a frontier lab; the good ending needs a model that scores 90 and is safe. Scaling rules and the best data mix are hidden per seed and estimated through in-game experiments. Playable from title to ending, packaged as a Windows executable; only the Steam store steps remain. A fresh take on the Hard Takeoff theme, sharing no code.

AI-assisted implementation. Balance figures come from bots, not human playtests, so they are not shown here.

GRILL ME 5.0

Simulation of an AI-native delivery pipeline

A discrete-event model of pull requests flowing through CI, human review and a merge train, with a rework loop. In the model, human review is the bottleneck at every load, and risk-based routing buys throughput at the cost of more latent defects reaching main.

All figures come from synthetic assumptions, not from a real repository. AI-assisted implementation.

AI-assisted implementationNo independent review
GitHub: not public yet

ExamShield

Multi-sensor check for AI smart glasses in exam rooms (research v0)

Combines an optical lens sweep, BLE, Wi-Fi header patterns and a visual check into one of three advisories for an invigilator, and always states which device types the checks performed cannot detect. It does not judge cheating, jam signals, read traffic or recognise faces.

Validated on synthetic data only; no real-device measurement yet. AI-assisted implementation.

GitHub: not public yet

생기로 (Saenggiro)

Evidence finder for Korean school records

Upload a school record and it detects the sentences that serve as evidence, then helps find study directions from that evidence. It does not write finished submissions for the user. The code is at beta level; the legal steps (terms of service, privacy officer, legal review) are not done, so it is not open.

AI-assisted implementation. Test counts in its status notes were not re-run for this page.
