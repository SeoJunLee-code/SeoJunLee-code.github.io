APTS-X Runtime Risk

Online calibration of HPC job walltime limits

In ProgressAI-assisted implementationNo independent review
Research questionWhen job runtimes drift, can an online-recalibrated upper bound keep the overrun rate near its target where a fixed 2× rule cannot?
Research prototype. It is not a scheduler or an energy optimizer. Only a 1,000-row public sample was audited; the main comparison uses synthetic drift.

Question

HPC users request a walltime limit for each job. If the limit is too tight the job is killed; if too loose the scheduler wastes room. This study asks whether a bound recalibrated online from recent jobs keeps the overrun rate near a target when runtimes drift, where a fixed rule such as “twice the estimate” does not.

Results Reported

Method (target 90%)Coverage after the shift
Fixed 2×49.87%
Rolling conformal88.79%
Adaptive conformal (ACI)89.83%
  • Synthetic drift, 8 seeds. The repository states the conclusion as “online recalibration beats fixed calibration”, not an effect unique to conformal methods
  • The change detector reset on average 0.625 times per seed and missed the change entirely in 3 of 8 seeds
  • With no delay in seeing finished jobs, rolling coverage right after the change rose from 61.8% to 76.5%: delayed feedback explains much of the dip
  • Public sample audit: of 1,000 rows only 549 job IDs are unique; one repeated ID adds 451 rows

Not done

  • The full public trace, a time-split evaluation and paired comparisons on the same trace
  • Handling of censored jobs (timeouts) and coupling to a scheduler simulator or energy metrics
  • Under drift, exchangeability fails, so coverage here is empirical, not a guarantee

Verification and AI use

Figures are quoted from the repository's report and checked against it; not re-run for this page. The repository reports 35 tests. An earlier “Pareto” claim was withdrawn after review. AI-assisted implementation; no independent review.
