Context Compaction — Does compaction save Claude Code usage?

Mixed Results

Does compacting the conversation context reduce Claude Code subscription usage without hurting task quality?

No saving on Sonnet 5.5 (0.96–1.52× full history); 0.33× on Haiku 5.5 above 100K tokens. Trimming the fixed per-call context mattered more.

Algorithm Innovation — A cache-line approximate membership filter

Mixed Results

With equal memory, can an insert-only filter that reads one cache line, with fingerprint length following block load, beat blocked Bloom and Prefix filter at a practical cost?

False-positive rate 0.117–0.695 of blocked Bloom at equal memory. Prefetching cut negative queries to 21.5–21.7 ns, but most speed goals were missed; the core idea was already published (TinySet).

AI Responsibility — Structural responsibility for AI agents

Hypothesis Refuted

Without presupposing consciousness or moral status, can AI agents combined with institutional structure perform the functions of responsibility?

The thesis is rejected as stated; what held up works without the agent: the deployer's liability and records.

AI Forgetting & Review — Eviction under a word budget; learned rehearsal rejected

Mixed Results

With storage and compute fixed, does one per-item need model that decides eviction and review together beat simple baselines at the same budget?

Content-aware eviction beats recency on real conversations (+9.7 pp out of sample, word budget); learned rehearsal gave no gain, and one large result was a length artefact.

AI Creativity — Doxa Negation Search

Hypothesis Refuted

Can AI create and develop creative ideas by extracting and negating the assumptions a population of ideas silently shares?

The pre-registered core hypothesis was rejected; a common critique-and-improve loop cut core retention from 5.00 to 1.88.

KORE — Knowledge Orchestration & Retrieval Engine

Prototype · Unvalidated

Can a retrieval layer pick the knowledge that actually helps a language model answer, and does it beat plain RAG under identical conditions?

No result yet. Nothing has been validated.

H1 — Majority-vote backfire

Hypothesis Refuted

When a model sometimes answers correctly, does majority voting fail on more than half of those problems?

Refuted in 4 of 4 conditions; backfire 14%–21%.

Paper 5 — Predicting success via internal signals

In Progress

Can an agent's early internal signal predict whether it will succeed, or does it only reflect how well it was trained?

An early AUROC of 0.820 is mostly explained by training setting k; a stricter follow-up found no detectable signal (0.548).

LLM Internal Computing — Verifying a deterministic computer inside an LLM

Reproduction · Partial

Do the unit tests of two upstream projects that embed a deterministic computer in an LLM pass at pinned commits?

First run: 246 of 282 and 479 of 562 tests passed. No performance claims.

Study 4 — World-model study

Exploratory · 3 seeds

The original report measures three things, blocked-move prediction, a position probe and a causal intervention, at 3,000, 12,000 and 24,000 steps in two environments (maze, empty).
