Research

Five lines of work. Each page states what was measured, what failed, and what was not checked.

Prototype · Unvalidated

KORE — Knowledge Orchestration & Retrieval Engine

Can a retrieval layer pick the knowledge that actually helps a language model answer, and does it beat plain RAG under identical conditions?

No result yet. Nothing has been validated.

Hypothesis Refuted

H1 — Majority-vote backfire

When a model sometimes answers correctly, does majority voting fail on more than half of those problems?

Refuted in 4 of 4 conditions; backfire 14%–21%.

In Progress

Paper 5 — Predicting success via internal signals

Can an agent's early internal signal predict whether it will succeed, or does it only reflect how well it was trained?

An early AUROC of 0.820 is mostly explained by training setting k; a stricter follow-up found no detectable signal (0.548).

Reproduction · Partial

LLM Internal Computing — Verifying a deterministic computer inside an LLM

Do the unit tests of two upstream projects that embed a deterministic computer in an LLM pass at pinned commits?

First run: 246 of 282 and 479 of 562 tests passed. No performance claims.

Exploratory · 3 seeds
