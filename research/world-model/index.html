What the report covers

Source: Drive report 「연구4_원본코드_확장_산출보고서」 (auto-generated 2026-10-27). Code: the project's original experiment script (TIER=CPU setting), changing only the step count and the output folder. The 3000-step rows were not retrained in that run; they are original results stored earlier (Drive wm_results).

  • A. Blocked-move prediction — headroom attained = (model − 6-gram) / (1 − 6-gram)
  • B. Position probe — classify the position after the action (pos_after); the layer where the linear probe scores highest
  • C. Causal intervention — per-layer net effect = flip along the mean-difference direction with the logit direction removed − flip along a random direction at the same α

C. Causal intervention Measured

Decision rule [J3] (fixed before the run; with n=3 it is only a comparison with the sd, not a test): a layer where mean net effect − 2×sd > 0. Result: maze, none at 3000, 12000 and 24000 steps. empty, none at 3000 steps and L2 at 12000 and 24000 steps.

[J2] Learning signal Measured

maze: the trained−untrained gap in subset linear accuracy went from +0.085 at 3000 steps to +0.196 at 12000 steps, a difference of +0.111 against a 2×sd criterion of 0.042, so the signal grew stronger as training converged. empty: +0.019 → +0.028, a difference of +0.010 against a 2×sd criterion of 0.013, so the change is within the sd range.

Limitations (from the report)

  • Three seeds, no significance tests
  • Not covered by this cell: the N=7 · T=64 GPU setting, a changed definition of the shortcut cap, runs beyond 24000 steps
  • The mean-difference direction is, as originally defined, the difference between ‘class means of the position before the action’. Time step t and position are mixed in it, so the L0 (embedding) effect may come from touching time-step information
